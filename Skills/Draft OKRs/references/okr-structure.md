## **The Rubric (What “Good” Looks Like)**

### **Hierarchy Rules**

**Level 1: Team OKRs (Leader-owned)**

* Owned by the sub-team leader  
* Focused on business outcomes, not tasks  
* Contain numeric targets (pipeline, SQLs, revenue, adoption, efficiency)  
* 5 Objectives max per team

**Level 2: Individual OKRs (IC-owned)**

* Must roll up cleanly into a single team Objective  
* Written by the IC as a planning and alignment exercise; Reviewed by leader and approved by VP  
* Translate outcomes into initiatives \+ delivery milestones  
* Explain *how* the team outcome will be achieved  
* Still measurable, but allowed to include execution gates  
* Limited to five Objectives per quarter, with one dedicated to professional development

**If an OKR cannot clearly answer “Which team Objective does this support?”, it does not belong.**

### **Objective Quality Bar**

A strong Objective must be:

| Criteria | Required |
| :---: | :---: |
| Outcome-focused | Yes (what changes, not what you do) |
| Time-bound | Yes |
| Directional, not a task | Yes |
| Understandable without context | Yes |

**Good Objective Examples**

| Good “Objective” Examples | What Belongs in “Key Results” |
| ----- | :---: |
| Refresh and expand email nurture programs to drive incremental SQL growth | Launch email nurture |
| Standardize paid media operating models to improve scale and predictability | Define cadence |
|  | Meet with XYZ\_Stakeholder |

### **Key Result Quality Bar**

Each Objective should have 2–5 Key Results, and every KR must meet at least one of these criteria:

| KR Type | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| Output | A concrete thing delivered (launch, framework, workflow) |
| Outcome | A measurable result (SQLs, leads, conversion lift) |
| Quality Gate | Approval, readiness, or enablement checkpoint |

**Key Results must be:**

* Binary or numeric (done / not done, X by Y date)  
* Specific enough that success is indisputable  
* Free of mixed intent (no “and” statements)

## **Canonical OKR Structure**

### **Team-Level OKR (Leader)**

**Objective:**  
Drive email performance to generate 135 SQLs by 3/31

**Key Results:**

* KR1: Generate 135 SQLs from email programs by 3/31  
* KR2: Improve nurture conversion rate from X% → Y%  
* KR3: Launch X net-new or refreshed nurture streams

### **Individual OKR (IC)**

### **Objective 1: Improve and expand lifecycle programs to drive incremental impact**

**Key Results:**

* KR1: Complete performance review and optimization plan by XDATE  
* KR2: Optimization plan reviewed and approved by stakeholders by XDATE  
* KR3: Launch updated core lifecycle program by XDATE  
* KR4: Launch at least one new segment- or vertical-specific lifecycle program by XDATE

### **Objective 2: Standardize operating procedures to improve consistency and scalability**

**Key Results:**

* KR1: Establish a standardized internal planning and communication process by XDATE  
* KR2: Define and document an annual operating cadence by XDATE  
* KR3: Launch a standardized framework for recurring programs and regional execution by XDATE  
* KR4: Operating model reviewed and approved by stakeholders by XDATE

### **Objective 3: Ensure reliable execution of run-the-business and priority initiatives**

**Key Results:**

* KR1: Deliver recurring business-critical programs on schedule throughout the quarter  
* KR2: Execute priority partner or stakeholder requests with 100% on-time delivery  
* KR3: Meet all agreed-upon SLAs for priority initiatives with zero misses
